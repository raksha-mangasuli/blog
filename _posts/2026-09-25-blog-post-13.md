This entire process of redesigning my blog site is so interesting and fun. So many decisions to make. The smallest of things take so much time. I'm having a really good time doing all of this.

Firstly, I decided to go with Buttondown for my newsletter subscription, but when I tried a sample, the emails & the entire process of signing up doesn't look that great, so need to check on it.
So many pros and cons to choosing between MailerLite and Buttondown. It's really tricky :(
Mailerlite has a nice email, but the positioning of elements is not that great, and too many repetitions of confirm your email in the email. Also when a user clicks on Subscribe on my site, it redirects to another site instead of confirming then and there that they are subscribed. Whereas Mailerlite has a very simple generic, confirm your email, but it lets the user stay on the page. But also Buttondown has these really cute gifs based emails when I get new subcribers which is really cute, but Mailerlite doesn't have these either. This whole process was pretty frustrating. I think I should take a break and get back to this in sometime. I'm leaning towards mailerlite because of no redirect links, unfortunately going to miss the cute gif emails of buttondown for new subscribers xD

I was trying to use AI tools to generate avatars today, tried Canva avatar maker, Figma, Google Nano Banana, Ilus.ai (this one was good, but ran out of credits very soon - maybe i can try creating such a site myself ), then lastly thought of trying it on recraft.ai (yet to try this one) but maybe I shouldn't spend so much time on just the avatar, let's see. None of the doodles, pixel arts were good, either I'm doing something wrong, or its actually difficult, not sure xD

Learning new things in UI design of screens, just adding dotted grids and normal grids makes the page so much more interesting and nice.

Finally something clicked now for the color palette of my portfolio and blog site, i cant have crazy colors for my portfolio site, blue is the most suited, but for my blog i can, i feel so good about this realization! It took so long to change the wallpaper and still make the rest of the elements color still in sync, so many possibilities & variations, you can easily get lost in this process.#

Redesign, migrations, all of these seem to add unnecessary complications, better to get it right the first time, but I guess it's never as straight forward as that xD

Finished the blog redesign now, it can't be exactly like how I want, but it's pretty good.

Next I have to rewrite the protfolio section. 
Its 25th sept today, planning to go live(as in just tell a few friends and family xD) and not think anymore that it's an unfinished site xD

The alignment of text and icons is taking way too long, I think I'll do the minute changes later. For now it looks pretty good <3
