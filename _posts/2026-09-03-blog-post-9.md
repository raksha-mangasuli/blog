Also you should really check out this grill-me skill by Matt Pocock, I used it and found it to be really useful: <https://github.com/mattpocock/skills> Alexander Eiswirth suggested this skill to me, so thanks to him, learnt something new & useful!
Here's a summary of the skill: Matt Pocock's "grill-me" is a skill for AI coding agents (like Claude Code) that forces the AI to interview you about a task before writing any code, asking one clarifying question at a time instead of guessing at your requirements. It walks through decisions like edge cases and scope, offering a recommended answer for each, and only proceeds once you've confirmed shared understanding. It's meant as a fast, lightweight planning step to catch ambiguity early, before it turns into wasted implementation work.
