You know, I've been damn worried about that I'm using LLMs to build my apps and sites. 

But I realised that the best outcome out of this is the best side effect: thinking about ideas all the time, especially about improving your product, getting even better ideas about new ones.

This is really exciting.

I think the first thing I want to do is to make my site/app of my blog so solid that I can write very easily and people can subscribe to it smoothly and this part of it can't change for a long time, even if other things change.

I had all these thoughts while watching Clarkson's farm, I just love the show, there's so much to agriculture, so much hard work and money involved, its probably one of the toughest jobs out there. I would definitely recommend you to go watch it. It's available on prime video right now, not sure where else. It might not look very interesting at a first glance, but trust me, you need to watch atleast an episode or two before deciding not to watch it. The show is by the popular host Jeremy Clarkson who hosted the popular show Top Gear about cars. I think the highlight of his show is his narration!
So my inspiration from the show was that, Jeremy keeps doing stupid things on his farm since he's never done these things before. He's making mistakes on a TV show and so damn confidently. I felt like that's it. My portfolio doesn't need to be perfect, my painting doesn't need to be complete, I can just document everything and blog about it. Isn't the process of doing things the main point anyways <3. Let's gooo!
